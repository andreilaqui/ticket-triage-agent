# Hotel Cancellation & Refund Policy

Applies to hotel room bookings at Northstar Lodge Canmore, Glacier View
Hotel Banff, and Columbia Trail Inn Golden only.

*Vacation rentals have a separate, stricter cancellation policy — see
`vacation-rental-policies.md`. Tours and shuttles have their own separate
policy — see `tours-shuttles-booking.md`. Do not apply this policy to
those bookings.*

## Standard Cancellation Window
| Timing before check-in | Refund |
|---|---|
| 5+ days | Full refund |
| 2–5 days | 50% refund |
| Less than 48 hours | No refund |

## Northstar Rewards Exception
Gold and Platinum tier members may cancel free of charge up to 24 hours
before check-in, regardless of the standard window above.

## Weather & Wildfire Exception
If Northstar closes a property or issues a mandatory evacuation notice
due to wildfire, severe weather, or highway closure, affected guests
receive a full refund or free rebooking, guest's choice — the standard
cancellation window does not apply.

## No-Shows
A guest who does not check in and does not cancel in advance forfeits
the full reservation cost, with no exceptions.
