# Summit Routes Tours & Shuttles

Summit Routes is Northstar Rockies' guided tour and shuttle service,
operating out of Canmore with pickup points at all three hotel
properties.

## Available Tours & Shuttles (current pricing)

| Tour / Shuttle | Price | Schedule |
|---|---|---|
| Banff Gondola Shuttle | $45/person round trip | Hourly, 8am-4pm, departs Northstar Lodge Canmore |
| Lake Louise Day Tour | $120/person (canoe rental add-on: $35/canoe, seats up to 2) | Departs 7:30am, returns ~6pm |
| Glacier Skywalk Excursion | $95/person | Half-day, Wed/Fri/Sun only, departs 8:30am, returns ~1:00pm |
| Columbia Icefield Adventure | $160/person, includes lunch | Full day, departs 7:00am, returns ~7:00pm |

*Prices last confirmed: [date]. Always quote from this table, not from
memory or a prior conversation - pricing changes seasonally.*

## Booking
Tours can be booked online, by phone, or at any hotel front desk. Payment
is collected at time of booking. Confirmation is sent by email with a QR
code for pickup.

## Cancellation & Refund Policy (Tours/Shuttles)
- 48+ hours before departure: full refund
- 24-48 hours before departure: 50% refund
- Less than 24 hours before departure: no refund

## Weather, Wildfire & Safety Holds
If Summit Routes cancels a tour or shuttle due to weather, wildfire smoke
advisories, or road closures, guests receive a full refund or free
rebooking, guest's choice - the standard cancellation window above does
not apply, regardless of how much notice was given.

## Group Bookings
Groups of 8 or more require 72 hours advance booking and a 25%
non-refundable deposit to hold the reservation.
