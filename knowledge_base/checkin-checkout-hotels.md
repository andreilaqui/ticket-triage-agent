# Hotel Check-In & Check-Out

Applies to all three hotel properties.

## Standard Times
- Check-in: 4:00 PM
- Check-out: 11:00 AM

## Early Check-In / Late Check-Out
Subject to availability. Requests can be made at booking or at the front
desk on arrival day.
- Early check-in (before 4:00 PM): no charge if the room is ready; a
  guaranteed early check-in can be pre-booked for $30
- Late check-out (after 11:00 AM, up to 2:00 PM): $30 flat fee; after
  2:00 PM, billed as an additional night

## Identification
A government-issued photo ID and a credit card matching the reservation
name are required at check-in. A $100 incidental hold is placed on the
card and released within 3–5 business days of check-out.

## Minimum Age to Book
Guests must be 21 or older to book and check in without an accompanying
adult.
