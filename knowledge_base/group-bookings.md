# Hotel Group Bookings

Covers room block bookings at the three hotel properties.

## Minimum Size
A group booking applies to 10 or more rooms booked together for
overlapping dates.

## Process
Group bookings must go through the sales team directly (not the standard
online booking portal) and require a signed group agreement.

## Deposit
A 20% non-refundable deposit is required to hold a group block, due
within 7 days of the agreement being signed.

## Attrition
If the group's final pickup falls below 80% of the blocked rooms, the
group is billed for the shortfall at the group rate.

## Cancellation
Full group cancellation more than 60 days before arrival forfeits only
the deposit. Cancellation within 60 days forfeits the full estimated
group revenue as calculated in the agreement.
