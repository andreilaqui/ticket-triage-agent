# Northstar Vacation Homes - Policies

Applies to Northstar Vacation Homes properties only. These are
self-contained homes, not staffed hotel rooms - several policies differ
meaningfully from the hotel properties.

## Check-In / Check-Out
Self-check-in via a keypad lockbox. The code is sent by email/text 24
hours before arrival. Check-in: 4:00 PM. Check-out: 10:00 AM, strict - a
cleaning crew is scheduled immediately after.

## Damage Deposit
A $300 damage deposit is pre-authorized on the guest's card at booking
and released 7 days after check-out if no damage is reported.

## House Rules
- No parties or events
- Quiet hours 10:00 PM - 8:00 AM
- Maximum occupancy strictly enforced per listing (varies by home)
- No smoking inside any unit ($250 cleaning fee if violated)

## Cancellation Policy
This is a stricter cancellation policy than hotel rooms, reflecting that
the whole home is held exclusively for one guest.

| Timing before check-in | Refund |
|---|---|
| 14+ days | Full refund |
| 7-14 days | 50% refund |
| Less than 7 days | No refund |

The Gold/Platinum free-cancellation exception does **not** apply here.

The same weather/wildfire exception that applies hotel-wide still applies
here: full refund or free rebooking if Northstar issues a closure or
evacuation notice.
