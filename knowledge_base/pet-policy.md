# Pet Policy

Policy varies by property and service line — check the specific business
line before confirming with a guest.

## Hotels
- Glacier View Hotel Banff: Pet-friendly. $40/night pet fee, maximum 2
  pets, 50 lb combined weight limit.
- Northstar Lodge Canmore: Pet-friendly. $40/night pet fee, no weight
  limit, maximum 2 pets.
- Columbia Trail Inn Golden: No pets permitted, service animals excepted.

## Vacation Rentals
Pet policy is set per listing, not company-wide. Some homes allow pets
with a $75 flat fee; others do not allow pets at all. Confirm the
specific property's listing before answering a guest.

## Tours & Shuttles
Pets are not permitted on any Summit Routes tour or shuttle. Trained
service animals are permitted with advance notice.

## Service Animals
Trained service animals are welcome at all properties and on all tours
at no charge, regardless of the pet policy above. This is separate from
"pet-friendly" status.
